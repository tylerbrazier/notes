--- Waterproofing ---
https://youtu.be/z_R0gEDZhAI
Need:
- Tube(s) of 100% silicone
- solvent (1 Gal is enough for 4L of fabric)
- calking gun
- knife to cut end of tube (if the gun doesn't have)
- rubber gloves (backup pair is handy)
- tarp to put down (it's a messy job)
- paper towels for cleanup
- container to measure out liters of fluid
- garbage bag
- 5 Gal bucket is handy
- clothesline to hang dry

Naptha & mineral spirits both work as solvent.

Video suggests 5:1 ratio of solvent:silicone
but I found that 4:1 is better (for more silicone in the fabric).
A standard 290ml (9.8floz) tube of silicone will be
1160ml (just over a liter) / 39.2floz of solvent.

Need enough solution to totally soak the fabric.

Give the solution time to dissolve;
look and see that there's no globs when you go to soak.

If soaking a bag, turn it inside out and soak both sides.

Fabric that touches other fabric will stick together;
you can pull them apart after drying but it leaves visual streaks (if you care)
